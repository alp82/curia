Dispatch

auto_dispatch?
On, curia starts the next takeable ticket by itself. Off, every dispatch is one you ordered.

How many agents may run at once. Each one costs a container, and three sandbox ports.
poll_interval_s?
How often curia reads the frontier of every watched repo. A save applies at once and cancels no running agent.
