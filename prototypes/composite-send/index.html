The composite send, mocked

ADR-0026 fixed the structure. One agent call returns an ordered array, each entry renders as its own Discord message with its own rigid format, and the deciding message posts last. At most four messages per send. What the structure left open is taste: the dressing, the order, the render form of the prose message and the question background, and how images and visuals carry intent. This page decides those, one round at a time.

Five rounds, and everything is decided. The small-print rail with plain labels, the prose cap at 1600, the conclusion first in bold with a list where the content is a list, the question background as small print behind 💡, one rule for when the agent composes, four image forms the agent picks between, and three visual fields in place of one. Section 14 lists every pick, each section carries the mark on the cut that won, and NOTES.md records every round. The record is the ADR-0026 amendment.

O1 Two prose messages, the agent picks the break

walk me through what parking actually does to a live pane, and what I lose

cu
curia APP09:14

1 of 4 · what parking does

Parking stops the process behind a pane and keeps the conversation. Three things happen, in order. The harness takes a stop signal and flushes its rollout to disk. The daemon records the conversation key, the model, the effort and the working directory. The tmux window closes.

None of the conversation is lost, because the conversation was never in the pane. It lives in the harness rollout file, which all four lanes write as they go. The pane is a view onto that file.

2 of 4 · what you lose

You lose the process state. A half-typed line in the composer goes. A tool call in flight is cut, and the harness records it as interrupted, so the next turn reads an interrupted call rather than a result. A shell the agent opened for itself dies with the window.

Rehydration reverses the first three steps and not the last one. The conversation comes back whole. The interrupted call does not re-run, so an agent that was mid-write returns to a file it half wrote, and it has to look.

A. A forced park. Every live pane parks, then rehydrates on the next boot.

13 The palette, and what binds it

What the five cuts draw from, and the rules that already hold. A variation that breaks one of these is not a candidate.

  • Titles. Discord renders #, ## and ###. None of them was tested in the render catalog of #414, so this page is their first judgment.
  • Small print. -# is measured and kept. ADR-0019 holds it to one line, never stacked, and every cut here obeys that.
  • The signal emoji. The set is fixed: ⚙️ ✅ ❌ ⚠️ 🎫 ⚰️ 🔗 ❓, with 🔎 for the cross-check and the six phase icons on the status line. Grade B prose refuses any emoji outside it, so no cut here invents one.
  • The code-block table and the ASCII diagram. Measured and kept, at most 42 columns, under 1000 characters. Every visual on this page fits both.
  • The deciding message posts last. ADR-0026. No cut moves it, and V5 reorders only the two messages above it.
  • Dressing is curia's, not the agent's. A rail curia writes takes no lint attempt. An intent line the agent writes does, which is the whole cost of V4. The picked rail splits the difference: curia writes the count, the agent writes the label at 20 characters.
  • The prose message caps at 1200 characters, and a question background at 600. Those are the two caps ADR-0026 added, and question 1 of round 2 is what happens at the first one.

14 What is settled, and what round 5 owes

Settled over four rounds. Every taste question the ticket named is decided, and so is every consequence the picks opened, except the two names of round 5.

  • The dressing. A small-print rail on every message of a send: curia writes the count, the agent writes a plain descriptive label at 20 characters, linted as inline text. A send of one message carries no rail.
  • The order. The deciding message posts last, as ADR-0026 fixed. Above it the agent orders by what the reader needs first.
  • The prose message. Cap 1600. The conclusion leads in bold, and the body under it is paragraphs or a list, chosen by what the content is.
  • The question background. Small print behind 💡, in one wrapped line or a stack, the agent's choice per question. This is the named exception to the never-stacked rule.
  • When to compose. A message exists when a reader would want to skip it on its own. Anything a reader always reads together stays one message.
  • The image. Four forms stand and the agent picks: on the card, its own message before the card, an ASCII diagram instead, or leading the send with prose reading it.
  • The arithmetic. The prose field caps at 1600 and CHUNK_LIMIT rises to 1700, so the rail always fits inside the message it labels. Past 1600 the agent composes a second prose message, and the refusal names that path.
  • The visuals. The visual message format stays and names a format only. The visual field retires. Three fields replace it, one per form the #414 catalog measured: picture, table and diagram, which the format also carries. images is renamed attachments.

Nothing is open. Five rounds settled every taste question the ticket named and every consequence the picks opened. The record is the amendment to ADR-0026, and the build lands with the handoff (#533).
