The composite send, mocked

ADR-0026 fixed the structure. One agent call returns an ordered array, each entry renders as its own Discord message with its own rigid format, and the deciding message posts last. At most four messages per send. What the structure left open is taste: the dressing, the order, the render form of the prose message and the question background, and how images and visuals carry intent. This page decides those, one round at a time.

Round 1 is decided. The small-print rail won on 2026-08-23, with the labels cut to plain descriptive words and no rail on a send of one message. Round 1 also opened a gap the operator named: text that will not fit one message. Round 2 is on the page in three questions, all under the picked rail. NOTES.md records every round.

curia APP09:14

1 of 4 · what parking does

Parking stops the process behind a pane and keeps the conversation. Three things happen, in order. The harness takes a stop signal and flushes its rollout to disk. The daemon records the conversation key, the model, the effort and the working directory. The tmux window closes.

None of the conversation is lost, because the conversation was never in the pane. It lives in the harness rollout file, which all four lanes write as they go. The pane is a view onto that file.

2 of 4 · what you lose

You lose the process state. A half-typed line in the composer goes. A tool call in flight is cut, and the harness records it as interrupted, so the next turn reads an interrupted call rather than a result. A shell the agent opened for itself dies with the window.

Rehydration reverses the first three steps and not the last one. The conversation comes back whole. The interrupted call does not re-run, so an agent that was mid-write returns to a file it half wrote, and it has to look.

6 The palette, and what binds it

What the five cuts draw from, and the rules that already hold. A variation that breaks one of these is not a candidate.

  • Titles. Discord renders #, ## and ###. None of them was tested in the render catalog of #414, so this page is their first judgment.
  • Small print. -# is measured and kept. ADR-0019 holds it to one line, never stacked, and every cut here obeys that.
  • The signal emoji. The set is fixed: ⚙️ ✅ ❌ ⚠️ 🎫 ⚰️ 🔗 ❓, with 🔎 for the cross-check and the six phase icons on the status line. Grade B prose refuses any emoji outside it, so no cut here invents one.
  • The code-block table and the ASCII diagram. Measured and kept, at most 42 columns, under 1000 characters. Every visual on this page fits both.
  • The deciding message posts last. ADR-0026. No cut moves it, and V5 reorders only the two messages above it.
