Choose the words of every scene of the final page — alp82/curia#587, on the map The curia landing page. Throwaway prototype, round 2.

The twelve scenes are locked by the storyboard. This page decides only the words. Tap a candidate and the panel above it shows the scene with that line in place. Each slot has a mine row: type your own line there and it becomes the pick. The copy button on a row copies that pick. The bar at the bottom copies every pick on the page in one block, ready to paste into the thread. Lines marked current are on the live page or in the storyboard today. Lines marked new go to the review gate. A dagger (†) marks a number the final build regenerates on build day.
